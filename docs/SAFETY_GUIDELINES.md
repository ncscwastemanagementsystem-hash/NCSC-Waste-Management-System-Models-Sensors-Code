# Safety Guidelines

This is a planning checklist, not a substitute for qualified engineering review or local regulations.

## Waste handling
- Isolate damaged/unknown batteries, aerosols, sharps, broken glass, medical waste, unknown chemicals and suspect hazardous items.
- Do not crush, heat, puncture, dismantle or open batteries/e-waste.
- Send batteries and e-waste through authorised collection/recycling channels.
- Never place hazardous items in a generic residual bin.
- Use supervised handling, suitable gloves/eye protection/closed footwear and handwashing.

## Machine safety
- Guard belts, rollers, pinch points and moving gates.
- Provide an accessible physical emergency stop and safe restart procedure.
- Protect circuits against overload, short circuit and accidental contact.
- Use properly rated power supplies and motor drivers.
- Provide dust/noise controls and stable mounting.
- Test jam, overload, sensor fault, full-bin and power-loss responses.
- Do not enable automatic operation before qualified review and supervised commissioning.

## Thermal or chemical recovery
Do not build improvised pyrolysis, burning, gasification or chemical-treatment equipment. Exhibition explanations should use a non-operating cutaway/model only. Real thermal or biological treatment requires expert design, suitable facilities, permits and monitoring.
